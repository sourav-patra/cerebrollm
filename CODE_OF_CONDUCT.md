# Code of conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews in this repository.

## What we expect

- Write about the work. Say what you observed, what you expected and how to reproduce it.
- Back a claim with a file, a command or a run record. Say "not measured" when you have none.
- Accept a correction that comes with evidence, and give one the same way.
- Assume a newcomer has not read every document. Link the page that answers the question.
- Keep private documents, credentials and personal data out of issues, pull requests and fixtures.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, or remarks about a person's identity, background or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Repeated off-topic posts, or advertising unrelated to the project.

## Reporting

Report a conduct problem privately to the maintainer by email: patra.sourav@gmail.com.

Include a link to the comment or pull request and say what happened. The maintainer keeps the reporter's name private unless the reporter agrees otherwise.

A security problem follows [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What the maintainer does

The maintainer reads each report and replies to the reporter. The maintainer may edit or hide a comment, lock a thread, or block an account from the repository. A person who disagrees with a decision can ask once, in private, for a second look.

Skipped or not checked: a reply-time target.
